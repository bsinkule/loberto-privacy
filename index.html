Loberto For Hire: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Loberto For Hire is a game for iPhone. This page explains what it does with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.

What stays on your phone

Your saved game (money, gear, jobs finished, settings) is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Internet use

The game does not need an internet connection and does not send your data anywhere. If you install it through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect crash reports and any feedback you choose to send, under Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
